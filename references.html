How it was built

Dosing was not written from memory. Structured Product Labels for every agent were retrieved from DailyMed, the National Library of Medicine's repository of current United States prescribing information, and the dosage and administration sections were transcribed. Where a drug has several labelled products, the innovator label was used: Keppra for levetiracetam, Lamictal and Lamictal XR for lamotrigine, Tegretol for carbamazepine, and so on.

Pharmacokinetic parameters and reference ranges come from the 2018 therapeutic drug monitoring review by Patsalos and colleagues, which is the source most laboratories' ranges derive from. Mechanisms follow the Sills and Rogawski review. Drug selection ordering follows NICE guideline NG217 as updated in January 2025. Status epilepticus doses are the American Epilepsy Society algorithm. Malformation rates are the EURAP registry figures.

The calculators implement the arithmetic printed in those labels and guidelines, nothing more. Each one displays the rule it applied beneath the result so you can check it.

Limits

  • Country. Doses reflect United States labelling and United Kingdom guidance. Licensed indications, available formulations and approved doses differ elsewhere. Check your national formulary.
  • Sulthiame has no FDA label, so no dose is given for it. Its reference range is quoted from Patsalos 2018 and its place in therapy from NICE.
  • Cenobamate, fenfluramine, ganaxolone and cannabidiol post-date the 2018 monitoring review and have no established reference range.
  • Prednisolone regimens for infantile spasms vary by region and are not given as a dose here.
  • The interaction checker covers antiseizure medications only. It does not include interactions with drugs used for other conditions, which are extensive for the enzyme inducers. Absence from the list is not evidence of safety.
  • The chooser annotates, it does not decide. Flags are drawn from labelling and registry data and attach to the guideline ordering. They do not re-rank it.
  • Currency. Compiled 11 September 2026. Labels change. Treat anything here older than your last label check as provisional.
