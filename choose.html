Negative selection

Drugs that worsen seizures

Seizure aggravation is a prescribing error that looks like treatment failure. When seizures increase after a new drug is started or a dose raised, suspect the drug before escalating it further.

Clinical settingAvoid or use with cautionWhat happens
Idiopathic generalised epilepsy with absence or myoclonic seizures Carbamazepine, oxcarbazepine, eslicarbazepine, phenytoin, gabapentin, pregabalin, tiagabine, vigabatrin Increased absence and myoclonic seizures; absence or myoclonic status epilepticus has been precipitated
Juvenile myoclonic epilepsy Lamotrigine, in a minority Myoclonus may worsen even while tonic-clonic seizures improve
Dravet syndrome (SCN1A loss of function) All sodium-channel blockers: carbamazepine, oxcarbazepine, phenytoin, lamotrigine Blocking NaV1.1 in inhibitory interneurons deepens the underlying defect; seizures increase
Any epilepsy, GABAergic agents Tiagabine, vigabatrin Non-convulsive status epilepticus, including in people without epilepsy taking tiagabine off-label
Lennox-Gastaut and Dravet on cannabidiol Cannabidiol without concomitant clobazam Seizure worsening was more common in trial participants not taking clobazam, possibly reflecting cannabidiol's own sodium-channel blockade

What the trials showed

Three results that shaped the ordering above.

SANAD II, 2021

Focal epilepsy

Lamotrigine gave a shorter time to 12-month remission than levetiracetam or zonisamide in newly diagnosed focal epilepsy. The findings did not support levetiracetam or zonisamide as first-line choices.

SANAD II, 2021

Generalised epilepsy

Valproate was more effective than levetiracetam in generalised and unclassifiable epilepsy. The clinical problem is that valproate is precisely the drug now restricted in anyone who might conceive.

Glauser, NEJM 2010

Childhood absence

Freedom from treatment failure at 12 months was 58% with valproate, 53% with ethosuximide and 29% with lamotrigine. Ethosuximide produced less attentional impairment than valproate, which is why it leads.
