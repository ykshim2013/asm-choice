Lamotrigine titration

The schedule changes completely with co-medication, and exceeding it is the main modifiable risk factor for serious rash. Pick the situation in front of you.

Two situations that catch people out

Starting or stopping an estrogen-containing contraceptive changes lamotrigine clearance roughly two-fold in either direction. Increase the maintenance dose when one is started, and reduce it by up to half when it is stopped.

If lamotrigine has been off for more than five half-lives, restart from the beginning of the schedule rather than the previous maintenance dose.
